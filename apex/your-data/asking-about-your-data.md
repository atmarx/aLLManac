## What FERPA gives you

<!-- Four rights, plainly, without statute-citation theater:
     - inspect and review your education records
     - ask to have records amended if you believe they are inaccurate or
       misleading
     - consent before the school discloses personally identifiable
       information, with exceptions
     - file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Education

     Then the correction that carries the page: there is no delete-my-data
     right in FERPA.  Students arrive expecting one because GDPR and CCPA
     have trained the instinct.  Knowing which regime grants which right is
     the actual literacy. -->

## Are chat conversations an education record?

<!-- Answer honestly: records directly related to a student and maintained
