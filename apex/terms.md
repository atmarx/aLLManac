{{PLATFORM}} is run by {{OPERATOR}}.  These terms were last changed on {{POLICY_DATE}}.

They cover {{PLATFORM}} as it is used for courses: each course's chat, and the {{PLATFORM}} chat with its guides.  A deployment set up for another purpose, such as a staff or research portal, has terms of its own.

Using {{PLATFORM}} means you agree to what is on this page.  It is written to be read in a few minutes.  What we collect and who can see it is on a page of its own: the [privacy notice](privacy.md).

## Who may use it

{{PLATFORM}} is for the people your institution's sign-in lets in.  Signing in gets you the {{PLATFORM}} chat and its guides.  A course's own chat opens only for the people on that course's roster, which its instructor keeps.

Your account is yours alone.  Do not sign in as someone else, and do not let someone else use your sign-in.

## What it is for

Coursework, teaching, and learning how these tools work, including building things with them.  Your institution's rules on acceptable use of its computing, and on academic integrity, apply here as they do everywhere else on campus.  So does your instructor's policy on AI in their course, which is the one that decides what you may hand in.

## What you agree not to do

- **Do not try to reach anyone else's work.**  That means other people's conversations, agents, files or keys, in your course or another.
- **Do not share your API key or use someone else's.**  A key is issued to one person for one course.  Use made with your key is counted as yours.
- **Do not try to get around a budget, a limit, or a setting** your instructor or the platform has put in place.
- **Do not put in material you are not allowed to share.**  Other people's private information, anything under a confidentiality agreement, and data your institution restricts do not belong in a conversation or an uploaded file.  If you are not sure, ask before you paste.
- **Do not use it to harass anyone, to break the law, or to produce material your institution's conduct rules forbid.**
- **Do not attack or overload the platform**, or use it to attack anything else.

If you find a way to do one of these by accident, that is a bug and we want to hear about it.  Any guide in the {{PLATFORM}} chat can file a report.

## Keys, budgets and usage

Each course has a budget, and each key has a limit of its own.  When a limit is reached, requests stop until it is raised or the period turns over.  Every request is recorded against your email address: which model, how many tokens, what it cost, and when.  You can ask the Usage Guide for your own record at any time.

## What a model's answer is worth

A model can be wrong, out of date, or confidently mistaken, and it can make up a source.  Check what matters before you rely on it.  What you submit, publish or act on is yours, whichever tool helped you write it.

Nothing a model or a guide says here is professional advice, and nothing it says is a decision by your institution.

Some courses use a model run by an outside provider.  That provider may refuse a request under rules of its own, and when it does, you are told.

## Your work

What you write, build and upload stays yours.  By putting it here you allow the platform to store it, back it up, and show it to the people the [privacy notice](privacy.md#who-can-see-it) lists.  Nothing you make is shared with classmates until you share it.

When you share an agent, the people you share it with can get at the files attached to it.  Attach only what you would hand them directly.

## No promise that it is always there

{{PLATFORM}} is run with care, and it is not guaranteed.  It goes down for upgrades, a model can be slow or unavailable, and a course's chat is closed and then archived after the course ends.  Backups exist to recover from a failed machine.  They are not a way to get back one thing you deleted.

Keep your own copy of anything you would hate to lose.  You can export your conversations and agents from a course while it runs and for 14 days after it closes.

## When access ends

Your access to a course ends when you leave its roster or the course is archived.  Your access to {{PLATFORM}} ends when your institution's sign-in no longer lets you in.  Access can also be suspended for breaking these terms, under your institution's own conduct process.

Ending access does not erase what you made.  The [privacy notice](privacy.md#how-long-we-keep-it) says what is kept and for how long.

## When these terms change

The date at the top changes when the terms do, and the current terms are always at this address.
