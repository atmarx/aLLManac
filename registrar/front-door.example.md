This platform is for teaching and coursework: classes, course projects, student clubs, and learning to build with AI.  Sponsored research — anything funded by a grant or contract, or involving research data — belongs in your institution's research computing environment instead, where the data agreements and support for it live.

Unfunded work that sits between the two, like a master's project that is also coursework, is welcome here; if you are unsure which side of the line you're on, say so in the request and a person will sort it out with you.
